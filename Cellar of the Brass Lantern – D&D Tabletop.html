Cellar of the Brass Lantern

A five-foot-grid skirmish following the D&D 5e (2014) basics

Click a green square to move your active hero. Click an enemy to attack: melee reaches adjacent squares, the longbow reaches 8 squares (disadvantage past that or with an enemy adjacent). Difficult terrain costs double. Walls block movement and sight.

Initiative
